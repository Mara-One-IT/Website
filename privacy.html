Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Mara One IT ("we," "us," "our") operates maraoneit.com and provides crypto trading bot deployment, blockchain infrastructure, and exchange platform hosting services ("Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information from visitors and clients.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: name, email address, Telegram handle, and any details you share when contacting us, requesting a quote, or engaging our Services (e.g. exchange API details, server access, infrastructure requirements).

Automatically collected information: standard web log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via our hosting provider and any analytics tools in use.

We do not collect: payment card numbers (payments are handled by third-party processors), or trading account passwords. API keys provided for trading bot deployment are used solely for the agreed service and never sold or shared.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver and maintain contracted Services (bot deployment, node maintenance, infrastructure hosting)
  • To respond to inquiries and provide support
  • To send service-related communications (e.g. maintenance notices)
  • To improve our website and Services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Infrastructure and hosting providers necessary to deliver the Services (e.g. cloud/VPS providers)
  • Third parties when required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

4. API Keys and Credentials

Where Services require exchange API keys or server credentials, we recommend clients issue keys with the minimum permissions necessary (e.g. trading-only, no withdrawal rights). Credentials are stored securely and used only for the scope of the engaged Service, and are deleted or revoked upon request at engagement end.

5. Data Security

We apply industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections, private networking (e.g. Tailscale), and firewall hardening, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide Services, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at office@maraoneit.com. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection law.

8. Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites (e.g. exchanges, Telegram). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: office@maraoneit.com
