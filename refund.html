Refund Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to services purchased from Mara One IT ("we," "us," "our"), including trading bot deployment, blockchain infrastructure, and exchange platform deployment/hosting Services.

1. Nature of Our Services

Our Services involve custom technical work — server provisioning, configuration, integration, and deployment — performed on a per-engagement basis. Because work begins promptly and often involves irreversible setup (e.g. server provisioning, API integration), refund eligibility differs from standard retail purchases.

2. Before Work Begins

If you cancel an engagement in writing before any work has started, you are entitled to a full refund of any deposit or advance payment made.

3. After Work Begins

Once work has started, fees for time and resources already spent are non-refundable. If the engagement is cancelled partway through, we will invoice or retain payment only for work completed up to the cancellation point, and refund the remainder where applicable.

4. Non-Delivery

If we fail to deliver the agreed scope of work due to fault on our part, you are entitled to a full or partial refund corresponding to the undelivered portion, at our discretion, or a redo of the work at no additional cost.

5. No Refunds For

  • Trading losses, missed profits, or strategy performance — we deploy and maintain infrastructure, we do not guarantee trading outcomes
  • Third-party exchange outages, API changes, or downtime outside our control
  • Change of mind after work has been completed and delivered as specified
  • Issues caused by client-side misuse, unauthorized changes, or failure to follow provided setup/maintenance guidance

6. Ongoing/Maintenance Services

For recurring or subscription-style maintenance and hosting Services, refunds for the current billing period are not provided once the period has started; you may cancel to stop future charges.

7. How to Request a Refund

Contact us at office@maraoneit.com with your engagement details and reason for the request. We aim to respond within 3–5 business days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The current version applies to engagements entered into after the "Last updated" date above.
