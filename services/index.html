Trading Bot Solutions

Mara One IT configures and deploys algorithmic trading bots — primarily with the Hummingbot framework — and runs them in production with monitoring and secure credential handling. That includes connecting to exchanges such as Coinbase Advanced Trade, Kraken, and Bitstamp, and building custom connectors when a trading pair or venue isn't supported out of the box. Read more about Trading Bot Solutions.

Blockchain Infrastructure

Running your own node gives direct, trust-minimized access to chain data instead of depending solely on third-party RPC providers. Mara One IT provisions the server, configures the node software, keeps it synced, and maintains it over time — alongside wallet and network infrastructure for the systems that depend on it. Read more about Blockchain Infrastructure.

Exchange Deployment

Exchange platforms are containerized with Docker, served through nginx, secured with SSL/TLS via Certbot, connected privately across servers with Tailscale, and hardened with UFW — then kept running as systemd units so they restart on failure. Read more about Exchange Deployment.

How the service lines fit together

Most engagements touch more than one area. A bot needs a hardened server to run on; an exchange platform needs blockchain connectivity for deposits and withdrawals; a trading system needs reliable chain data. The full tool and platform breakdown is on the About page, and common questions are answered on the FAQ page.
